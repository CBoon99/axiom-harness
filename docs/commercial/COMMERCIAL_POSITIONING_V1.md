## What it is (one sentence)

**Run AI experiments under controlled conditions, preserve exactly what happened, and produce an auditable sealed record.**

## What it is not

Axiom Harness is **not** a toy, internal developer harness, generic AI playground, unfinished research framework, feature demo, or “just another agent UI.”

It is a **governed experimental / evidence execution environment**.

## Commercial value (not features)

The value is the combination:

**CONTROL + ISOLATION + REPEATABILITY + PROVENANCE + MULTI-MODEL/MULTI-ACTOR EXECUTION + RECEIPTS + SEALED RESULTS**

A serious buyer does not buy “lots of cool rooms.” Rooms are machinery. The value is that experiments are **defined, frozen, run isolated, compared, and sealed** — not left as informal conversation logs.

## Buyer proposition

Axiom Harness lets an organisation:

- define an experiment,
- freeze the conditions,
- run isolated actors/models,
- capture what each actor saw and did,
- preserve failures and unknowns,
- and seal the resulting evidence with provenance.

## Core language (use these)

**CONTROLLED, BOUNDED, REPEATABLE, PROVENANCE-BACKED, FAIL-CLOSED, SEALED, AUDITABLE**

Avoid: truth, consciousness, hallucination elimination, perfect behaviour, guaranteed compliance, “scientifically valid merely because Harness ran.”

## Why this positioning

AI experiments are often difficult to reproduce, compare, audit and explain after the fact. Harness does not make an experiment scientifically valid — it provides the **controlled execution and evidence layer** so the organisation can inspect what happened, under what conditions, with which actors, producing which outputs, and preserve the result.
